🌼 Daisy Tasks

A living garden where your tasks grow. Each task you add becomes a little plant that grows through multiple stages as you water it (complete it). When you're done, pick it and add it to your vase.

Daisy Tasks was built because I kept trying different productivity tools and trackers, but what I really needed was a gentle reminder that met me where I was. Not only is Plant growth a lovely metaphor, but I wanted to add a little fun to stressful moments. I'm currently drawing flowers to replace the current ones, and please submit bug reports or feature requests so I can make this a better experience for you!

Built with care for everyone who wants to try a gentler way to grow.
